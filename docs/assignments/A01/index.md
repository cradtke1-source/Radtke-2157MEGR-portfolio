Product analysis

a. The primary mechanical function of the extendable box cutter is to transmit a controlled cutting force from the user’s hand through a sliding blade so that a thin, hardened edge can cut sheet materials (cardboard, tape, plastic film) while the unused portion of the blade remains enclosed inside the handle for safety and to maintain durability.


b. The dominant behavior during cutting is a shear process at the blade edge, but the force the user must apply is governed by the simple lever action at the contact point:
force provided by the user X distance from grip center to the blade tip = cutting resistance force at the material X length of blade extending beyond the housing. 

When the blade is retracted or extended, the change in length of blade extending beyond the housing directly changes the mechanical advantage.

d. I used patent example US10994428B2– “Retractable utility knife”
Inventor: Robert Joseph Gallegos, Thomas John Scimone, Scot Herbst
Two alternative solutions that perform the same primary function are, a fixed-blade utility knife with blade permanently exposed, and a snap-off segmented blade knife where the blade advances by breaking off dull segments rather than sliding a single blade.
The original engineer chose an auto-retracting spring-loaded carrier instead of a pure friction lock. I think this decision was made in the name of safety. The blade returns inside the housing as soon as the cutting load is removed, reducing the chance of cutting oneself. 

## Decide
A person viewing this portfolio needs to understand immediately that it is a professional working record of engineering design decisions, not a personal website or collection of finished products. Thus the homepage states the portfolio’s purpose and the standard it holds itself to. This information is placed first so that a grader or viewer can orient themselves, evaluate subsequent entries and move through the site without having to search for context or interpret my intentions. The side headings act as a way to any other section of the portfolio directly from the home page in one or two clicks.

I changed the heading color of my portfolio to yellow. I did this to better represent the mix of colors that symbolize UNCC, it's not just green but also gold/yellow and white. The previously green header with the mostly green banner photo left too much green visually present. Addditionally the specific shade of bright green not being exactly like the classic UNCC dark green caused a bit of visual disharmony that I believe the yellow fixes.

Every assignment entry will be completed on time without last-minute crunching and will, to my best effort, follow instruction accurately, contain a clear statement of the decision or problem, the criteria and constraints used, the alternatives considered, a justified selection supported by the available evidence, and a brief note on remaining uncertainties or lessons learned.
