# A10 – Lead Screw Translating System

## Lead screw design - Cooper Radtke
Our goal is to design and build a mechanically driven tensile tester capable of applying up to 2 kN axial tensile load with a controllable crosshead speed from 1 to 50 mm/min. This is to be done with 2 screws that are at minimum 18 inches long. All calculations must use a saftey factor of four.
