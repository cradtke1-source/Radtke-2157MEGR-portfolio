About Me
Cooper Radtke

I am a mechanical engineering student developing my skills in engineering design, problem solving, analysis, and technical communication. I chose mechanical engineering because it is the most broad school of engineering. Mechanical engineering combines mathematics, physics, creativity, and hands-on problem solving, which makes it a field that fits the way I approach challenges. Through my coursework and projects, I am learning how to move from an initial problem or requirement to a design that can be analyzed, tested, and improved.

As I continue through my mechanical engineering education, I hope to become an engineer who values the reasoning behind a design just as much as the final result. I want to be able to explain why I selected a particular material, geometry, manufacturing method, or design approach rather than simply presenting the finished product. I am developing experience with engineering analysis, CAD, technical documentation, and communicating technical information to other people. I also recognize that engineering decisions involve constraints and assumptions, so I am working to identify those factors and understand how they affect the validity of my designs. My goal is to become an engineer who can approach unfamiliar problems systematically, support decisions with evidence, and communicate technical work well enough that another engineer can understand and build upon it.


To defend an engineering decision means being able to back up a particular design choice with calculations, evidence and engineering principles. At this point, I understand the basic idea of defending a decision, basic , low stakes decsisions but I do not think I can consistently do it for tougher, more sophisticated or nuanced decisions at the level expected of a practicing engineer. I can explain some of my easier choices, but I need more experience connecting more important choices to quantitative evidence and explicitly stating the assumptions that make my reasoning valid. I expect this skill to improve throughout the semester as I practice documenting my design process and evaluating my decisions.
